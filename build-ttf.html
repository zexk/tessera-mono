Each pixel becomes one CCW square contour; the renderer's non-zero fill rule handles adjacent pixels. Vertical metrics follow the bitmap's row table — baseline at row 10, ascender 11 rows up, descender 5 rows down, totalling the 1024 UPM grid declared in the spec.
